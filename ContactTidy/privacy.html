我們處理哪些資料

ContactTidy 是 Android 通訊錄整理工具。為了列出、比對、合併與整理你的聯絡人，App 必須在裝置上讀取與修改系統通訊錄，因此需要 Android「聯絡人」權限（READ_CONTACTS、WRITE_CONTACTS）。App 不要求其他危險權限，也不要求網路權限。

App 在你的裝置上讀取的通訊錄資料包括：

  • 聯絡人的姓名各欄位（例如姓、名、中間名）與電話號碼。
  • 每筆聯絡人所屬的帳號來源（帳號類型與名稱）。此資訊是從系統通訊錄的聯絡人資料中讀取，App 不會另外讀取裝置上的帳號清單。

目前版本會在你的裝置本機儲存：

  • App 偏好設定，例如排序方式與篩選條件。這些設定不含聯絡人內容。

App 不會另外保存你的聯絡人副本；你的聯絡人仍由 Android 系統通訊錄保存。

匯出功能（規劃中，推出後適用）：未來版本預計提供匯出功能。只有在你主動選擇匯出，並透過 Android 系統檔案選擇器指定檔案名稱與儲存位置後，App 才會將聯絡人資料寫成 JSON 或 VCF 檔案到你指定的位置。App 不會自動匯出，也不會把檔案傳送給開發者。

資料如何使用

上述資料只用於：

  • 依帳號來源列出聯絡人並供你編輯。
  • 比對不同來源的同一位聯絡人並顯示差異，在你確認後合併。
  • 依你的操作批次整理姓名，例如將中間名併入名字，或合併名字只有一個字元的聯絡人姓名。
  • 依電話或任一姓名欄位排序，依文字或欄位字元長度篩選。
  • 記住你的 App 偏好設定。
  • （匯出功能推出後）將你選擇的聯絡人寫入你指定的檔案。

資料分享

目前版本不包含帳號、廣告、分析 SDK、當機回報 SDK 或雲端同步，也沒有網路權限。ContactTidy 不會將你的聯絡人或任何其他資料傳送給開發者或任何第三方，也不提供將聯絡人分享給其他 App 的功能。

所有處理都在你的裝置上進行。依 Google Play 的定義，只在裝置上存取與處理、從未傳送出裝置的資料不屬於「收集」，因此本 App 在 Google Play「資料安全性」區段聲明不收集、不分享任何資料。

你在 App 中所做的編輯與合併會直接寫入系統通訊錄。若聯絡人屬於有同步功能的帳號，Android 系統與該帳號服務提供者可能依你的設定同步變更；此同步由系統與服務提供者處理，適用其隱私權政策，並非由 App 傳送。

匯出功能推出後，匯出檔案存放在你自己選擇的位置。若你選擇的位置屬於雲端儲存服務，檔案的上傳與保存由該服務依其條款與隱私權政策處理，開發者無法存取。

ContactTidy 為 Google Play 付費下載 App。付款由 Google Play 處理，適用 Google 的條款與隱私權政策；App 本身不會存取你的付款資訊。開發者可能透過 Google Play Console 取得 Google 提供的訂單資料（例如訂單編號），僅用於處理退款與帳務。

資料保存與刪除

你的聯絡人保存在 Android 系統通訊錄中，不由 App 另外保存。App 偏好設定保存在你的裝置上，直到你清除 App 資料（「設定 → 應用程式 → ContactTidy → 儲存空間 → 清除資料」）或解除安裝 App。解除安裝 App 不會刪除你的聯絡人，也不會還原你已套用的修改。

App 已停用 Android 自動備份（allowBackup=false），App 資料不會透過 Android 備份上傳。

匯出功能推出後，你匯出的檔案由你自行管理，可隨時在檔案所在位置刪除。

你可以隨時在「設定 → 應用程式 → ContactTidy → 權限」撤銷聯絡人權限。開發者的伺服器上沒有任何屬於你的資料，App 也沒有帳號註冊功能，因此不需要另外申請刪除資料。

安全

資料不會離開你的裝置，安全性主要仰賴 Android 系統的權限與 App 沙箱機制。App 的修改會直接寫入通訊錄，建議你在大量整理前先備份通訊錄。匯出功能推出後，匯出檔案含有聯絡人資料，請存放在你信任的位置。

兒童隱私

ContactTidy 不是專為兒童設計的 App。若未來的目標受眾或資料處理方式改變，將依 Google Play 要求更新揭露內容。

What data we process

ContactTidy is an Android tool for organizing your address book. To list, compare, merge, and tidy your contacts, the app must read and modify the system address book on your device, so it needs the Android Contacts permission (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS). The app requests no other dangerous permissions and no network access.

Address book data the app reads on your device includes:

  • Contact name fields (such as family, given, and middle name) and phone numbers.
  • The account source (account type and name) of each contact. This is read from the contact data in the system address book; the app does not separately read the list of accounts on your device.

The current version stores on your device:

  • App preferences, such as sort order and filter settings. These contain no contact content.

The app does not keep its own copy of your contacts; they remain in the Android system address book.

Export (planned; applies once released): A future version is planned to offer export. Only when you choose to export and pick a file name and location through the Android system file picker will the app write contact data as a JSON or VCF file to that location. The app never exports automatically and never sends the file to the developer.

How data is used

This data is used only to:

  • List contacts by account source and let you edit them.
  • Compare entries for the same person across sources, show the differences, and merge them after you confirm.
  • Batch-tidy names at your request, such as moving the middle name into the given name, or combining the names of contacts whose given name is a single character.
  • Sort by phone number or any name field, and filter by text or field character length.
  • Remember your app preferences.
  • (Once export is released) Write the contacts you choose to the file you specify.

Data sharing

The current version has no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or cloud sync, and no network permission. ContactTidy does not send your contacts or any other data to the developer or any third party, and has no feature for sharing contacts with other apps.

All processing happens on your device. Under Google Play's definition, data that is accessed and processed only on the device and never transmitted off it is not "collected", so the app declares in its Google Play Data safety section that it collects and shares no data.

Edits and merges you make are written directly to the system address book. If a contact belongs to an account with sync enabled, the Android system and that account's provider may sync the change according to your settings. That sync is handled by the system and the provider under their own privacy policies, not transmitted by the app.

Once export is released, exported files are stored where you choose. If you choose a location provided by a cloud storage service, uploading and storing the file is handled by that service under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

ContactTidy is a paid download on Google Play. Payment is processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; the app itself never accesses your payment information. The developer may receive order data provided by Google through Google Play Console (such as an order number), used only for refunds and accounting.

Data retention and deletion

Your contacts are stored in the Android system address book, not separately by the app. App preferences stay on your device until you clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > ContactTidy > Storage > Clear data) or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not delete your contacts or undo changes you already applied.

Android Auto Backup is disabled for the app (allowBackup=false), so app data is not uploaded through Android backup.

Once export is released, files you export are yours to manage and can be deleted at any time from where they are stored.

You can revoke the Contacts permission at any time in Settings > Apps > ContactTidy > Permissions. There is no data about you on the developer's servers and no account registration, so no separate deletion request is needed.

Security

Your data never leaves your device, so its security relies mainly on Android's permission system and app sandbox. Because changes are written directly to your address book, we recommend backing up your contacts before large clean-ups. Once export is released, exported files contain contact data; please store them somewhere you trust.

Children's privacy

ContactTidy is not designed for children. If the target audience or data handling changes in the future, disclosures will be updated as required by Google Play.

We may update this policy because of new features, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements, and will update the "Last updated" date on this page. If any data will ever leave your device, this policy will be updated before that feature is released, and you will be notified in the app beforehand.
